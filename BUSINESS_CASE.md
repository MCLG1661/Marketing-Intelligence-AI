# Business Case Study — Marketing Intelligence

## Business challenge

Marketing teams need to connect campaign data and strategic objectives to actionable decisions.

## Solution

AI agent grounded in the documented MIA methodology, covering analytics, strategy, content, SEO and commercial intelligence.

## Competencies demonstrated

Marketing analytics, prompt engineering, KPI interpretation and strategic planning.

## Documented evidence

Five documented functional scenarios cover the agent's main competencies.

## Scope and limitations

Scenario-based evidence is not a guarantee of accuracy or commercial results; no automatic integration with MIA.
